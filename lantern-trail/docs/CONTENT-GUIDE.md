# The Lantern Trail: Content Guide

Every devotional, in every age band and language, is written and reviewed against this guide.
If a piece of content conflicts with this guide, the guide wins.

> **Our aim:** that every child knows God as their Father, Jesus as their friend and Saviour,
> and the Holy Spirit as the One who lives in them, and that they learn to hear Him and
> love Him back.

---

## 1. What we believe

> **Doctrinal basis:** the teaching of **Rhema Bible Church** (South Africa): its statement of faith
> (Appendix A) and the *rhema* teaching that God speaks personally today. Where this summary and the
> statement differ, the statement wins. Topics to weave in deliberately: the new birth, faith, healing,
> the gifts and power of the Holy Spirit, and speaking God's Word.

1. **The Bible is God's Word.** It is true and trustworthy, and it is how God reveals Himself to us.
2. **God is one God, existing eternally in three Persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.**
3. **God created people in His own image.** People sinned, and sin brought physical and spiritual
   death. Everyone is born with a sinful nature, which leads to wrong choices and real guilt.
4. **Jesus is the Son of God and God in the flesh,** fully God and fully human. He died in our place
   as a substitutionary sacrifice for our sins, and everyone who believes in Him is made right with God
   (justified) through His shed blood.
5. **Jesus rose bodily from the dead and ascended into heaven,** where He now prays for us as our
   High Priest and speaks for us as our Advocate.
6. **Jesus is coming back,** personally, and it could be soon.
7. **The new birth:** everyone who repents of their sins and receives Jesus by faith is born again by
   the Holy Spirit, becomes a child of God and has eternal life.
8. **God is our Father.** He made us for relationship and wants us to live as His children.
9. **Through the cross we have open access to God's presence.** The curtain is torn
   (Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:19–22). Children can come close to God at any time, just as they are.
10. **We are saved by grace through faith,** not by being good enough (Ephesians 2:8–9).
11. **The Holy Spirit lives in everyone who follows Jesus.** He helps, comforts, leads and empowers us.
12. **God speaks today.** He speaks through His written Word, and personally by His Spirit (the
   *rhema* word: the living word God speaks into a person's heart). What God says personally
   always agrees with the Bible and sounds like His love.
13. **Obedience flows from love.** We keep God's commandments because we love Him and want to
   please Him (John 14:15; 1 John 4:19), never to earn His acceptance. We do not neglect God's law;
   we see it as the good way of a loving Father.

## 2. The Five Lights

These are the five truths every trail keeps returning to. Each night should clearly connect to
at least one of them, and every expedition should touch all five.

| # | Light | In a child's words |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **God is my Father** | "I am a child of God. He loves me and is proud to be my Dad." |
| 2 | **Jesus is my friend and Saviour** | "Jesus is God who came close. He died and rose for me. He is my best friend." |
| 3 | **The Holy Spirit lives in me** | "God lives in my heart and helps me." |
| 4 | **God speaks, and I can hear Him** | "God talks to me through the Bible and in my heart, and I can listen." |
| 5 | **I obey because I love Him** | "I do what God says because I love Him, not so He will love me. He already does." |

The nightly closing words in every band: **"I am a child of God and a friend of Jesus!"**

## 3. Tone: relationship, not performance

### Always
- Lead with **who God is** (His character), then **who I am** to Him, then **how I respond**.
- Present God's commands as the **good way of a loving Father**, like a parent's safety rules.
- Present sin honestly but gently, and **always close the loop with grace in the same night**:
  sin → God comes looking → forgiveness → restored closeness.
- Frame obedience as a **response to love**: "Because God loves you so much, you can…"
- Speak identity over the child: "You are His child", "God is proud of you", "God delights in you".
- Make God's presence **warm and safe**: He is close, He hears, He welcomes.
- Present the **return of Jesus** as the happiest day ahead: we will see our Friend face to face.
  It is a hope, never a threat.
- Explain **sin** as something every person has inside (not just "bad kids"), and Jesus as the
  One who takes it away and gives a brand-new heart (the new birth).
- Encourage **listening to God**: short quiet moments, with the guardrail that God's voice
  always agrees with the Bible, always sounds like love, and is good to share with a parent.

### Never
- Fear-based motivation: "God will be angry / stop loving you / punish you if…"
- Performance or earning language: "Be good so God will love you", "God only listens to good kids",
  "Earn God's blessing".
- Shame: "You should be ashamed", "Bad boy/girl", comparing children to each other.
- Making missions or progress a test of acceptance. Missions are invitations, not requirements.
- Treating the law as worthless, or presenting God as permissive about sin. Grace is not "anything goes".

### Product rules that follow from this
- **No streak shaming.** Missing nights never loses progress, breaks a streak or triggers guilt
  messages. The trail simply waits ("Welcome back! Your lantern is still lit.").
- Collected treasures are **things we discovered about God**, not rewards for being good.
- No leaderboards or comparisons between children.

## 4. Age bands

All five bands follow **the same Bible story on the same night**, each at its own level.

| Band | Who leads | Time | Story length | Bible text | Nightly parts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2–3** | Parent | 3–5 min | 80–140 words | One short simple-wording phrase, repeated | Lantern on · Story · Verse (with action) · One truth · Action or song · 1–2 line prayer · Goodnight words |
| **4–5** | Parent | ~10 min | 250–380 words | Simple-wording verse | Lantern · Story · God's Word · 3 treasures · 2 questions · Repeat-after-me prayer · Mission · Goodnight words |
| **6–7** | Parent and child | 12–15 min | 300–450 words | Simple-wording verse **+ look it up in a real Bible** | All of 4–5 · *Did you know?* · *Think deeper* · Journal prompt · Prayer read together |
| **8–9** | Child reads, parent joins | ~15 min | 400–600 words, child reading level | **Real passage (BSB), 3–8 verses** | Read the passage · Story retelling · Treasure · 3 questions incl. one application · Listen moment (1 min quiet) · Own prayer with prompt · Journal · Mission |
| **10–12** | Child alone | 15–20 min | 400–700 words | **Real passage (BSB), 8–15 verses** | Read · Reflect (what does this show about God? about me?) · Listen moment (2 min) · Journal · Own prayer · Mission · Weekly parent check-in question |

### Band notes
- **2–3:** one idea only. Concrete, sensory, repetitive. Include a body action ("arms wide like
  the sea opening"). No frightening details: Goliath "fell down", not injury; Noah's flood is about
  being safe in the boat.
- **4–5:** sound effects and drama are welcome. Hard topics (the cross) are handled gently and
  always end in hope.
- **6–7:** introduce simple Bible navigation (book, chapter, verse), fun facts, and "why" questions.
- **8–9:** begin real Bible reading. Questions move from recall to meaning to "what will I do?".
- **10–12:** speak to the child directly as a capable follower of Jesus. Invite honest questions
  and doubts. Teach them how to hear God personally (journalling what they sense, testing it
  against Scripture, talking it over with a parent or leader).

## 5. Scripture policy

- **English quotations:** the **Berean Standard Bible (BSB)**, which is public domain. Bands 8–9 and
  10–12 quote it directly.
- **Simple wording:** bands 2–3, 4–5 and 6–7 may use child-friendly paraphrase. Show it as
  "simple wording" with the reference, so families can look it up.
- **Afrikaans:** use our own simple wording for now. *Open item: confirm which Afrikaans
  translation may be quoted commercially before bands 8–12 quote a translation directly.*
- Always use the reference numbering of the language's own Bible (for example Isaiah 9:6 in
  English is Jesaja 9:5 in Afrikaans).
- Never put words in God's mouth that are not in Scripture. Where a story imagines detail, it
  must be clearly imaginative ("Maybe Jesus laughed with them…").

## 6. Language and personalisation

- **English** uses international/UK spelling (colour, Saviour, Mom stays "Mom").
- **Afrikaans** is written natively, not translated word for word. Use Afrikaans Bible book names.
- The child's name and pronouns are filled in from their profile. Use the tokens in
  `content-model.ts` (`{name}`, `{subj}`, `{obj}`, `{poss}` and their capitalised forms) rather than
  writing "he" or "she" in parent-facing text.

## 7. Review checklist (every night, before publishing)

- [ ] Scripture is accurate, and the reference is correct for this language.
- [ ] Connects to at least one of the Five Lights.
- [ ] No fear, shame or performance language (section 3).
- [ ] Sin, if mentioned, is followed by grace in the same night.
- [ ] Obedience is framed as a response to love.
- [ ] Right length and parts for the band (section 4).
- [ ] Age-appropriate handling of anything frightening.
- [ ] Pronouns use tokens, so it works for boys and girls.
- [ ] Afrikaans reads naturally.
- [ ] A reviewer has signed it off.

## Appendix A: Rhema Bible Church statement of faith

*Summary as provided by the project owner (12 November 2026). Replace it with the official wording if needed.*

- **The Bible:** the Scriptures are the inspired and authoritative Word of God.
- **The Trinity:** God exists eternally in three Persons: the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit.
- **Creation and sin:** God created man in His own image, but man sinned and incurred physical and
  spiritual death, inheriting a sinful nature that leads to actual transgression and personal guilt.
- **Substitutionary sacrifice:** the Lord Jesus Christ died for our sins as a substitutionary sacrifice
  according to the Scriptures, and all who believe in Him are justified through His shed blood.
- **Resurrection and ascension:** the bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ, His ascension into heaven,
  and His ongoing ministry as High Priest and Advocate.
- **Return of Christ:** the personal, imminent return of the Lord Jesus Christ.
- **New birth (regeneration):** all who repent of their sins and receive Jesus Christ by faith are born
  again of the Holy Spirit, becoming children of God with eternal life.

### How this shows up for children
| Belief | Where it appears on the trail |
|---|---|
| Creation and sin | Days 1–3 (made in God's image; the garden; God comes looking) |
| Substitutionary sacrifice | "Jesus' Greatest Rescue" (took our place) and the Christmas "Best Present" night |
| Resurrection, ascension, High Priest | "He Is Alive!", "The Helper Comes" (Jesus is in heaven praying for us) |
| Return of Christ | The final night of every journey cycle: "Jesus is coming back", told as joyful hope |
| New birth | The invitation prayer on the last night of each trail, explained age-appropriately |
