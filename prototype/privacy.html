Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

VDW Health and Safety Solutions (Pty) Ltd ("VDW", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, how we protect it and what your rights are under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who is responsible for your information

VDW Health and Safety Solutions (Pty) Ltd is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website.

  • Information Officer: Niel
  • Email: niel@vdwsafety.com
  • Phone: 072 843 8094
  • Address: Pretoria, South Africa

2. What information we collect

When you complete the free Multi-Branch Compliance Check:

  • Your name, role, company name, phone number and work email address.
  • Information about your company: number of branches, number of employees, type of business and whether your clients audit you.
  • Your answers to the check questions, and the score and gaps worked out from them.

When you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, number and message.

Technical information: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to run and secure the website. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

3. Why we use your information

  • To show you your compliance check results and send you a copy.
  • To contact you about your results and, if you wish, about our services.
  • To answer your questions and arrange consultations.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

We process your information with your consent (the tick box on the compliance check), to take steps you have asked for, or for our legitimate business interests where this does not override your rights. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing niel@vdwsafety.com.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information or share it with anyone for their own marketing. We only share it with service providers who help us run this website and our business, such as our website host and email provider. They act as operators on our behalf and may only use your information to provide their service to us.

Some of these providers store information on servers outside South Africa. Where this happens, we only use providers that are bound by data protection laws or agreements that give a level of protection substantially similar to POPIA.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above. Compliance check submissions and enquiries are deleted no later than 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a client or the law requires us to keep them longer.

6. How we protect it

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access limited to the people who need it.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • Ask whether we hold personal information about you, and request a copy of it.
  • Ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date or that we are no longer allowed to keep.
  • Object to us using your information, including for direct marketing.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

To use any of these rights, email niel@vdwsafety.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
