# VDW Messaging Guide

This guide sets out who the website speaks to, what problem it solves and the words to use. The prototype pages now follow it.

**Lead with the problem, not the client type.** Headlines speak to the audit scramble, scattered documents and lack of visibility across branches. "Suppliers to industrial & mining clients" appears in supporting lines (eyebrows, "Built for" strip, FAQ), not as the headline.

---

## 1. Ideal client

**Who:** a company with **multiple branches** that **supplies products or equipment to industrial and mining clients**. Examples: equipment suppliers, industrial distributors, hire companies and parts suppliers.

**Where they are with safety:** they have some safety in place, or none, but **no system**. Documents sit in different branches, on people's laptops and in old files. Each branch does things its own way.

**Who decides:** the **MD or CEO**, sometimes an Operations Director.
**Who feels the pain day to day:** branch managers, admin staff, or a SHEQ coordinator if they have one.

**What usually triggers the call:**
- An industrial or mining client announces a **supplier audit** or vendor compliance check.
- A tender or new contract asks for SHE documentation.
- A failed audit, a lost contract or an incident.
- Opening a new branch.
- The MD realises they can't answer "Are we compliant?" for every branch.

## 2. The problem, in the client's own words

Use this language on the site. It's how they describe it, not how a consultant would.

- "When a client audits us, we run around like headless chickens trying to find documents."
- "Every branch does it differently."
- "The documents are somewhere, but nobody knows where."
- "I don't actually know where we stand on safety."
- "We only fix things when an audit is coming."
- "Tracking compliance across all our branches is impossible."

**The real cost:** lost time before every audit, a risk of failing a client audit and losing a big contract, inconsistent standards between branches, and an MD who is personally exposed under the OHS Act without knowing it.

## 3. Old way vs. new way

| The old way | The VDW way |
|---|---|
| Documents spread across branches, laptops and files | **One centralised system** for every branch |
| Each branch does its own thing | **One standard** applied across all branches |
| Panic and late nights before every client audit | **Audit-ready on demand:** find any document in minutes |
| MD finds out about problems during the audit | **MD sees each branch's status at a glance** |
| Safety is reactive, fixed when someone asks | Safety is **managed and tracked every month** |

## 4. Positioning statement

> VDW Health & Safety Solutions is the **outsourced safety department for multi-branch companies**. We manage your health & safety compliance at every branch, **using our own VDW Compliance Control System**, so you're ready whenever your industrial and mining clients audit you, and your MD always knows where each branch stands.

**Important:** VDW doesn't hand clients a system to maintain themselves. VDW runs the system and manages the client's compliance with it. The copy says "we manage it for you", "on our system" and "your outsourced safety department". It never implies the client's team does the upkeep.

## 6. The three core messages (value props)

1. **One central system.** Every document for every branch is in one place, organised the same way. No more hunting.
2. **Audit-ready on demand.** When a client asks for your safety file, appointments or risk assessments, you have them in minutes, not days.
3. **Visibility for leadership.** A branch-by-branch dashboard shows the MD what's compliant, what's expiring and what needs attention.

## 8. Words to use and avoid

**Use:** branches, centralised, one system, client audit, supplier audit, audit-ready, industrial and mining clients, MD, at a glance, every branch, on demand, up to standard.

**Avoid or use less:** generic "warehousing, engineering & manufacturing" as the main audience, consultant jargon, "paperwork" as the selling point, and fear-only messaging. Lead with control and confidence; fines come second.

## 9. Objections to answer (FAQ)

- "We already have someone doing safety." → We give them a system and a standard to work from across all branches.
- "We have documents, they're just all over the place." → That's exactly who this is for. We centralise and standardise what you have and fill the gaps.
- "Our branches are in different areas." → *(Confirm how VDW handles distance: on-site visits, remote support or both.)*
- "How long does it take to get all branches on one system?" → *(Need a real timeframe.)*
- "What happens when our mining client audits us?" → You pull the documents from one place, and we can help you prepare.

## 10. Decisions made

- **The system:** VDW manages clients' compliance on its own system, the **VDW Compliance Control System**. Use the full name a few times per page (hero, services, about), then "our system". It isn't shown on the site, and clients aren't promised a login. Documents remain the client's and are sent on request.
- **Case study:** approved. The current client appears as an anonymous case study ("Equipment supplier to industrial & mining clients · Multiple branches"). **The results still need real figures**, confirmed with the client.
- **Coverage:** nationwide. Head office in Pretoria, with on-site visits to branches across South Africa and remote support in between.
- **Audience:** companies that supply products or equipment to industrial and mining clients. The focus is on the problem solved.
